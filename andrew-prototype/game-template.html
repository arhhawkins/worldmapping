Atlas of Time

Start in 3400 BCE, when the first cities and the first writing appear, and unlock history one step at a time.

  1. Explore the glowing pins on the map.
  2. Play challenges to earn ✦ Insight. Get them right first time for the most.
  3. Spend Insight to move time forward and watch the world change.
  4. Tap any state to see what it was like, with pictures, and stamp your passport.

This is a prototype. The stories and questions are drafts that still need fact-checking.
